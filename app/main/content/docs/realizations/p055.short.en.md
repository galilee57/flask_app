## The project

Combine process expertise, advanced statistics, and artificial intelligence to identify non-quality factors.

In industry, the occurrence of a quality defect on a production line does not necessarily mean its origin lies in that same facility.

A defect may result from manufacturing parameters located several steps upstream, complex interactions between variables, or sporadic phenomena difficult to identify with classical methods.

## My contribution

My intervention covers, among other things:

- Understanding and formulating industrial problems.

## Results

These studies enabled reducing rejection rates related to the analyzed defects.
