## The project

Production must take place in a clean and controlled environment.

Controlling surface cleanliness is a major issue in the manufacturing of steel products intended for demanding applications.

An industrial production line encountered surface defects identified under the name "Black Spots", associated with deposits of dust and particles.

## Results

- Collateral effects on other defects with common mechanisms.
