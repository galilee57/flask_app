# Lead the reorganization of lockers and shift changes during the Covid-19 crisis

## About this project

At the beginning of the Covid-19 crisis, an industrial site had to maintain its essential activities while quickly applying new health requirements.

Lockers represented a critical point: several teams used them daily, especially during shift changes, with risks of personnel concentration in sometimes cramped spaces.

The diversity of rooms, the arrangement of lockers, access to showers and sanitary facilities, as well as shift schedules made it impossible to apply a uniform solution.

**I was appointed cross-functional reference for locker and shift management, with responsibility for analyzing risks and defining operational adaptations.**

## Objectives

- Ensure compliance with distancing rules in lockers.
- Secure circulation and access to collective facilities.
- Quickly deploy understandable and applicable rules on the ground.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Analyze risks in each locker room

I carried out a detailed analysis of different locker configurations.

I formalized the results in analysis tables to distinguish high-risk areas and define necessary measures.

### 2. Map occupancy and circulation constraints

I produced representations of the rooms to visualize spatial constraints using MS Visio.

These tools allowed determining usable areas, locations to neutralize, and circulation possibilities.

The objective was to transform health requirements into concrete rules adapted to each configuration.

### 3. Define reorganization measures

Based on the risk analysis, I contributed to defining solutions adapted to different locker rooms:

- Limitation of the number of people present simultaneously.
- Condemning certain lockers or locations.
- Reassigning personnel to other rows or rooms.
- Modification of circulation directions.
- Implementation of signage and markings.
- Restrictions on use of certain areas.
- Presence of references during shift periods.

These measures allowed addressing the specific constraints of each area rather than applying general rules that were insufficiently adapted.

### 4. Follow up actions and adapt the setup

I contributed to formalizing and tracking the selected measures.

The follow-up documents allowed identifying necessary actions and their progress status.

## My Role

**I served as the cross-functional reference for managing lockers and shift changes within the Covid-19 crisis management framework.**

The physical implementation of accommodations and other aspects of crisis management were collectively handled by the various site stakeholders.

## Results

**Structured analysis of lockers:** identification of constraints and risk situations for different rooms.

**Adaptation of shift changes:** implementation of organizational measures aimed at limiting gatherings.

**Operational implementation:** production of plans, rules, and follow-up documents, with several actions recorded as completed.

## Lessons Learned

The diversity of configurations required designing local responses rather than applying a single standard.

**This experience illustrates my ability to take charge of a cross-functional issue in a crisis situation, structure an operational response, and quickly adapt the industrial organization to new constraints.**
