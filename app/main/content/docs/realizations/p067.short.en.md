## The project

Lead an OPR approach for workshop transformation

The workshop - specialized in repair and finishing operations - faced difficulties related to the accumulation of work-in-progress (WIP) and the saturation of its storage spaces.

This situation resulted from several interdependent factors: incoming volumes, non-conformities, repair capacities, facility availability, logistics organization, and physical product management.

## My contribution

**I led the workshop reorganization approach and coordinated cross-functional actions necessary for improving flows and reducing work-in-progress.**
