# Renovating and Adjusting a Mountain Bike: Mechanics, Components, and Maintenance

## Context

I undertook a personal project to completely renovate a mountain bike (MTB).

This approach included purchasing parts, interventions on the wheels and brakes, as well as various mechanical adjustments.

Beyond the renovation itself, this project provided an opportunity to develop my practical knowledge of how a bicycle functions, the compatibility of its components, and the operations necessary for its maintenance.

## Objectives

- Renovate an MTB and its main equipment.
- Perform the necessary mechanical adjustments.
- Deepen my skills in mechanics and maintenance.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Identify Renovation Needs

I approached the project by examining the MTB's equipment and identifying the interventions required.

### 2. Source and Purchase Components

I sourced and purchased the parts intended for the renovation.

### 3. Perform Mechanical Adjustments

I carried out adjustments as part of the renovation.

## My Role

**I undertook and led this personal mountain bike renovation project, taking charge of sourcing and purchasing the parts as well as performing the mechanical adjustments.**

## Technologies and Methods

**Technical Areas:** mechanical components, part compatibility.

**Methods:** renovation, maintenance, component sourcing, mechanical adjustment, functional diagnosis and control.

## Results

- Completion of a personal project renovating a mountain bike.
- Development of practical knowledge in bicycle mechanics.

## Lessons Learned

### Adjustments are an integral part of a renovation

Replacing or restoring components must be accompanied by appropriate adjustments and checks.

**This project illustrates my interest in mechanics, my ability to undertake technical renovations, and my willingness to deepen my skills through practice.**
