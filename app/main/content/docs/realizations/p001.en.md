# Lead an international benchmark to develop managerial practices and team cohesion

## Context

Within the framework of a multi-month management training program, I participated in a collective benchmark project with an industrial operations team abroad.

This approach had a dual dimension: discovering and comparing professional practices in another industrial environment, while developing the managerial skills of the participants.

The project also provided an opportunity to strengthen links between team members by mobilizing them around a common objective and deliverables intended for Industrial Management.

**I was appointed head of this team project**, with the mission to organize its execution and coordinate collective work.

## Objectives

- Organize a benchmark with an operations team abroad.
- Discover other industrial operating and management practices.
- Structure a collective approach within the framework of training.
- Distribute responsibilities among team members.
- Plan work and track progress.
- Build common deliverables.
- Present the approach to Industrial Management.
- Strengthen cohesion and collaboration among participants.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Take responsibility for the collective project

I was designated as project manager within our management training program.

I coordinated its organization, participant coordination, and work tracking.

### 2. Organize and lead the team

I organized the distribution of roles among participants, taking into account the various activities to be carried out.

I ensured management and coordination of the team during the project.

The project thus served as a practical application of the lessons from the management training program.

### 3. Plan and track work

I defined and followed the organization of activities necessary to carry out the benchmark.

This approach included planning different stages, tracking progress, and coordinating contributions.

### 4. Prepare the benchmark content

I participated in defining the study themes and deliverable contents.

The benchmark was based on an exchange and comparison approach with an industrial operations team located abroad.

This confrontation with other professional practices provided an opportunity to broaden our understanding of industrial operating modes and organization.

### 5. Prepare the presentation to Industrial Management

I coordinated the preparation of deliverables and the final presentation.

The work produced was delivered and presented to Industrial Management.

This presentation marked the culmination of the collective project, allowing us to share the approach followed and the lessons learned from the experience.

### 6. Strengthen team cohesion

Beyond the benchmark content, the project played an important role in consolidating relationships between participants.

Preparing, exchanging ideas, and achieving a common goal allowed the team to get to know each other better and develop collaboration habits.

This human dimension constituted one of the main contributions of the experience.

## My Role

**I was appointed project manager and ensured managerial and organizational oversight.**

## Methods and Skills

**Areas:** industrial management, international benchmarking, industrial operations, managerial training.

**Methods:** project management, distribution of responsibilities, planning, progress tracking, collaborative work, reporting to management.

**Skills:** leadership, team coordination, organization, communication, coordination, synthesis, team cohesion.

## Results

- Conducted a benchmark with an industrial operations team abroad.
- Managed a collective project within the framework of multi-month managerial training.
- Organized and coordinated team contributions.
- Produced expected deliverables.
- Presented the approach to Industrial Management.
- Strengthened bonds and cohesion among participants.

## Lessons Learned

### 1. A shared goal strengthens cohesion

Working on a common project fosters mutual understanding and builds lasting professional relationships.

### 2. Benchmarking allows stepping back to reflect on one's own practices

Exploring other industrial organizations encourages questioning one's habits and considering different ways of working.

**This experience allowed me to concretely exercise project management responsibilities, lead a team in an international context, and measure the importance of a common goal in building collective cohesion.**
