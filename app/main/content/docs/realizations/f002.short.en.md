# Specialized Mastère in Maintenance Management — Arts et Métiers (ENSAM)

## Education

I completed the Specialized Mastère in Maintenance Management at ENSAM. The brochure describes a one-year programme with **380 hours of classes, case studies and site visits**. It allocated 155 hours to maintenance management and 225 hours to maintenance techniques and operations.

Modules included dependability, financial analysis, inventory and contracts, maintenance engineering, component behaviour, decision support and human factors.

## Industrial study and thesis defence

The programme included an industrial study lasting more than six months in a company, culminating in a **professional thesis defended before a panel**.

## Professional thesis — Preventive maintenance plan at Saint-Fons Chimie

My thesis focused on critical machinery at the **Rhône-Poulenc Saint-Fons Chimie site in France**. I reviewed vibration analysis, inspection rounds and oil analysis, then used a simplified FMEA to select priority equipment. The work involved site staff and external specialists, including Bently Nevada, Brüel & Kjær and Campagna. It included building a team trained in preventive maintenance and proposing investments.

I set up training on a physical test rig that reproduced mechanical faults. The team used it to practise collecting and analysing vibration measurements.

## Career context

This programme was a foundation for my career. I applied its methods in my maintenance roles and in work on equipment reliability and cost planning.
