# Deploy and generalize mobile terminals to secure industrial traceability

## About this project

On an industrial production line, traceability, identification, and product control operations relied on procedures requiring data entry and verification via the production computer system.

These practices presented several risks: re-entry errors, inconsistencies between the physical product and its digital identification, trips to the control cabin, and late detection of certain anomalies.

To improve operational reliability, I initiated the deployment of industrial mobile terminals directly on the shop floor.

The goal was to bring digital inspections and product validations closer to the physical product while securing exchanges with the industrial system.

## Objectives

- Digitize traceability operations directly on the line.
- Secure product identification.
- Limit re-entry errors.
- Enable real-time information validation.
- Reduce unnecessary operator movements.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Deploying the first terminals

This first step aimed to enable operators to perform traceability operations directly in contact with products, without relying systematically on the control cabin.

**This initial deployment formed the foundation for the progressive rollout of trackers along the line.**

It enabled a new way of working based on digital data entry and validation as close as possible to the operations themselves.

### 2. Extending digitization to other process stages

After deploying the terminals, the project evolved toward expanding their usage.

### 3. Strengthening integration with the industrial system

The operation of mobile terminals relied on exchanges with the production computer system.

This integration allowed verification and validation of information directly from the terminals.

The terminal thus became a genuine operational tool, integrated into the line's functioning.

## My Role

**I initiated and implemented the use of mobile terminals.**

## Results

- Successful implementation
- Strengthened risk control
- Sustainable integration into standards

## Lessons Learned

### Gradual deployment facilitates transformation

The initial installation at the line entry allowed introducing the technology before expanding its use to other process stages.

### Integration with industrial systems is essential

A mobile terminal becomes truly useful when it enables verifying and validating information directly within the production process.

**This project demonstrates my ability to initiate digital innovation on the shop floor, organize its gradual deployment, and contribute to its generalization to enhance traceability and quality control of an industrial line.**
