## The project

Digitize in-line inspections

On an industrial production line, traceability, identification, and product control operations relied on procedures requiring data entry and verification via the production computer system.

These practices presented several risks: re-entry errors, inconsistencies between the physical product and its digital identification, trips to the control cabin, and late detection of certain anomalies.

## My contribution

**I initiated and implemented the use of mobile terminals.**

## Results

- Strengthened risk control

- Sustainable integration into standards
