## The project

Install a continuous measurement device, characterize its performance, define its calibration, and operate its data

## My contribution

**I contributed to the implementation and qualification of the device, intervening at the interface between instrumentation, metrology, data analysis, and process control.**

The project was carried out collectively with instrumentation, maintenance, and process teams.

## Results

The device enabled having an indicator of surface cleanliness before and after degreasing.

Studies confirmed the system's ability to differentiate certain cleanliness situations, while identifying precision limits and the influence of product families.
