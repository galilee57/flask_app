# Lead the digitalization of an industrial handling equipment

## About this project

In a metal product transformation industrial environment, repair and compliance operations mobilized several installations.

A main line ensured the bulk of the treatments, while complementary equipment allowed certain operations to be performed without systematically mobilizing that line.

These alternative means represented an important lever to improve flow fluidity, limit bottlenecks, and adapt product routes to the operations actually required.

In this context, a modernization project for a handling equipment had been initiated.

The initial objective concerned replacing an aging equipment with safety limitations. The scope then evolved towards a broader ambition: **making the new equipment programmable and integrating it into a more digitized industrial operation**.

## Objectives

The project pursued several complementary objectives:

- Integrate the equipment into production and handling processes.
- Improve control of operations performed on products.
- Facilitate the operation of this equipment as a complementary means to the main line.

Beyond digitizing a machine, the challenge was to contribute to better organization of treatment flows.

## Approach

### 1. Take over a struggling IT project

The hardware modernization project had already been initiated, but its IT extension no longer had a sufficiently clear organization.

I re-established contact with the concerned stakeholders to reconstruct the file history, identify work already completed, and clarify responsibilities.

### 2. Bridge industrial needs and IT teams

Digitizing a handling equipment requires translating field constraints into needs understandable by IT teams.

I worked with the concerned stakeholders to advance this aspect and align operational expectations with technical implementation possibilities.

**My intervention was situated at the interface between industrial users, technical managers, and IT teams.**

### 3. Support the transition to programmable equipment

The project aimed to go beyond the simple replacement of a handling means.

The new installation was required to benefit from computer functionalities adapted to its operations.

Hardware preparations included, among other things, the integration of a screen and a printer at the workstation level.

### 4. Coordinate hardware and IT dimensions

One of the classic difficulties of this type of project lies in synchronizing several disciplines.

Mechanical and electrical works can advance independently of IT developments, while their final operation depends on their integration.

I contributed to this coordination so that the IT aspect could progress in coherence with the modernization of the installation.

### 5. Reintegrate the equipment into the global organization of flows

The interest of the equipment went beyond its individual operation.

It was part of several complementary industrial means capable of performing certain repair or compliance operations.

**The approach thus combined digital transformation of an equipment with a global understanding of its role in industrial performance.**

## My Role

I took an active part in relaunching and digitizing the handling means, in collaboration with the IT teams.

I intervened on the functional and cross-functional dimension of the project, complementing the teams responsible for the design and technical realization of the equipment.

## Results

**Digitization of an industrial handling means, carried out in collaboration with IT teams.**


## Lessons Learned

**This project illustrates my ability to lead a digital transformation right at the operational level, by linking industrial expertise, cross-functional coordination, and collaboration with IT teams.**
