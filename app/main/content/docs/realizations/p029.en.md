# Reconstruction of a control bench after a fire

About the project

After the fire, the objective was to rebuild the bench and restore its capacity to perform expected inspections in operations. Budget: €1M.

## Assessing damages and defining what needs to be rebuilt

- Organizing subcontractor teams for manual inspection (ensuring service to the end customer)
- Securing the area and establishing an inventory. The diagnosis covers the bench structure, measurement equipment, controls, and their connections.
- This inventory allowed us to decide which elements to keep, restore, or replace. The inspections that the bench must ensure serve as a starting point for defining the functions to be restored and the criteria that will allow them to be verified.
- Taking improvements into account: to prevent recurrence of the fire (sprinklers), production voted on, and maintenance of the area.

## Reconstruction site

- Validation of the schedule and obtaining the committed budget from Management
- Negotiation with suppliers of inspection machines
- Organization of the construction site

## Acceptance and commissioning

- Technical acceptance
- Training of operators and subcontractors
- Product qualification
- Transfer / stop of manual activity and ramp-up to production

## Lessons Learned

- **Start from the inspections to be restored.** Defining the expected functions of the bench and acceptance criteria from the outset guides choices regarding restoration or replacement.
- **Prepare for acceptance during the diagnosis.** The test protocol must ensure the safety of the bench, its capacity to perform required inspections, and the repeatability of its measurements under planned operations conditions.
