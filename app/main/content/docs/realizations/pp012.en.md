# Exploring music through the practice of piano and guitar

## Context

Alongside my professional activities and technological projects, I am interested in music and practice two instruments: piano and guitar.

These two instruments offer different approaches to music, both in their technique and their possibilities for expression.

This personal activity allows me to explore an artistic field where listening, coordination, precision, and practice occupy an essential place.

## Objectives

- Develop my musical knowledge and practice.
- Work on the coordination and precision of gestures.
- Deepen my understanding of rhythm, melodies, and harmony.
- Maintain an artistic activity complementary to my technical projects.
- Take pleasure in learning and playing music.

## Approach and Practice

### 1. Practicing Piano

I practice piano to explore its musical possibilities.

This instrument allows me to approach music through a keyboard and discover the relationships between melody, chords, and harmony.

### 2. Practicing Guitar

I also practice guitar, which offers a different instrumental approach.

It allows me to explore chords, rhythms, and melodies through a stringed instrument.

This second practice enriches my discovery of music and allows me to approach complementary instrumental techniques.

## My Role

**I undertook a personal approach to learning and practicing piano and guitar.**

This activity reflects my interest in music and my willingness to develop skills in an artistic field.

## Fields and Skills

**Instruments:** piano, guitar.

**Fields:** instrumental practice, musical listening, rhythm, melody, harmony.

**Transferable Skills:** coordination, concentration, patience, precision, perseverance, artistic sensitivity.

## Results

- Personal piano practice.
- Personal guitar practice.

## Teachings

### 1. Two instruments offer complementary perspectives

The piano and guitar allow for exploring different ways to produce and organize sounds.

### 2. Musical progression relies on practice

Learning an instrument requires regularly revisiting gestures and pieces to deepen them.

### 3. Artistic activities enrich a technical career path

Music adds a creative and sensitive dimension complementary to engineering and software development activities.

**Piano and guitar practice illustrates my curiosity for artistic disciplines and my interest in learning new skills beyond my professional field.**
