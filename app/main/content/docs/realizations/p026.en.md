# Lead a project for industrial storage and coordinate technical teams

## About this project

An industrial storage project requires coordinating multiple roles with complementary responsibilities: new works, responsible for aspects related to new installations; local operations, responsible for operational constraints; and maintenance, ensuring the consideration of maintainability and reliability requirements.

In the context of a storage tank project, I led a **multidisciplinary team** bringing together these different functions.

This project also involved a budgetary dimension, with responsibility for the budget within my maintenance scope.

**Objective: Coordinate technical and operational actors of the project, ensure coherence of their interventions, and control maintenance budget commitments.**

## Approach

### 1. Lead a multidisciplinary team

I coordinated a team bringing together three essential functions:

- **New works**: addressing challenges related to the realization of new installations.
- **Local operations**: integrating operational constraints and site needs.
- **Regional maintenance**: representing maintainability requirements and equipment reliability issues.

My role consisted of organizing exchanges between these actors, aligning their needs, and ensuring coherence of decisions made within the project framework.

### 2. Align technical and operational requirements

Managing an industrial storage project requires reconciling sometimes conflicting priorities:

- Technical requirements associated with the installation.
- Site operational constraints.
- Maintenance needs and equipment accessibility.
- Safety conditions related to the industrial environment.
- Economic and organizational constraints of the project.

I served as the interface between different roles to ensure their requirements were taken into account in project management.

### 3. Lead the maintenance budget aspect

I was responsible for the budget within my maintenance scope.

This responsibility involved monitoring financial commitments associated with this scope and integrating budgetary constraints into project trade-offs.

Management thus combined technical coordination with economic responsibility.

### 4. Coordinate contributions from various stakeholders

The project's success relied on the ability to get teams operating at different organizational levels to work together.

I played a central role in this coordination, linking site-level issues, new construction contributions, and regional maintenance responsibilities.

This organization enabled a cross-functional approach to the project rather than a succession of independent interventions.

## My Role

I led the **project team**, with direct responsibility for the maintenance budget.

My main responsibilities:

- Leading and coordinating the multidisciplinary team.
- Interface between new construction, local operations, and regional maintenance.
- Considering technical and operational constraints.
- Representing maintenance issues.
- Monitoring the budget within my scope.
- Coordinating contributions and making decisions across different business functions.

This mission allowed me to exercise cross-functional leadership by involving teams with complementary expertise and objectives.

## Results

Construction and delivery of storage facilities within schedule and budget constraints

## Lessons Learned

**This project demonstrates my ability to lead a cross-functional technical team, coordinate multiple organizational levels, and assume budgetary responsibility in an industrial investment project.**
