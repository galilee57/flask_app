# Digitizing my library to build a personal knowledge base with AI

## Context

I owned a personal library of about 500 books, built up over the years, representing a collection of readings, knowledge, and areas of interest.

I engaged in reflection on how to preserve, organize, and valorize this documentary heritage through digital technologies.

My project was to gradually digitize this library to constitute a personal documentary corpus, with the perspective of making it queryable thanks to artificial intelligence.

In the long term, I wish to explore the possibilities of **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to retrieve information, connect ideas, and query the contents of my library.

## Objectives

- Gradually digitize the works in my library.
- Build an organized digital documentary fund.
- Preserve and make content accessible in a usable format.
- Study document processing and structuring techniques.
- Prepare a semantic search architecture.
- Explore the possibilities of RAG applied to a personal corpus.
- Facilitate information retrieval and idea connection between works.
- Eventually have a personal tool for exploring my readings and areas of interest.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Define the documentary scope

The project takes as its starting point my personal library.

The challenge consists in identifying the works involved and reflecting on their organization in a future digital fund.

### 2. Digitize the works

Digitization took place over the years and moves!

The acquisition of several automatic scanners allowed us to industrialize this phase.

### 3. Prepare corpus structuring

Building an exploitable documentary database is not limited to accumulating files.

It requires reflecting on the organization of works, their metadata, and the quality of digitized content.

The envisaged steps include notably:

- Identification of works and their characteristics.
- Organization of files.
- Optional text recognition via OCR.
- Quality control of extracted content.
- Structuring of documents and their metadata.

### 4. Study a RAG architecture

The second ambition of the project is to make the documentary corpus queryable in natural language.

The proposed approach relies on **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, which combines the retrieval of relevant passages from a documentary corpus with response generation by a language model.

This architecture would allow searching for information in works without relying solely on the model's general knowledge.

At this stage, this is a technical perspective, not an already operational system.

### 5. Explore new forms of documentary search

Beyond traditional book consultation, a RAG system could enable:

- Retrieving passages dedicated to a particular topic.
- Identifying works addressing common themes.
- Comparing viewpoints.
- Exploring relationships between different readings.
- Obtaining answers accompanied by references to source documents.

The project could thus become a tool for exploring my own reading journey, without claiming to automatically deduce my personal motivations.

## My Role

**I initiated this personal digitization project and defined the ambition of transforming my library into a corpus exploitable by artificial intelligence.**

## Technologies and Methods

**Technologies envisaged:** document digitization, OCR, Python, text processing, embeddings, vector databases, language models, RAG.

**Methods:** corpus constitution, documentary structuring, indexing, semantic search, data engineering, AI experimentation.

## Current Status and Results

The project has a clearly defined ambition: to constitute a personal digital documentary heritage and prepare its exploitation via a RAG architecture.

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Digitization is a documentary engineering project

Transforming physical works into digital data requires reflecting on formats, text quality, and information organization.

### 2. RAG connects artificial intelligence to a documentary heritage

This approach allows considering answers based on identified documents rather than solely on the general knowledge of a model.

### 3. A personal project can become a technological experimentation ground

The library constitutes a concrete use case to deepen my skills in documentary processing, semantic search, and generative artificial intelligence.

**This project illustrates my commitment to combining personal culture with digital technologies, gradually building the foundations of a knowledge base that can be queried by AI.**
