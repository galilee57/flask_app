## The project

Design personal tools to transform videos into structured notes and automatically organize photo libraries.

## My contribution

**I led the development of these personal applications, from needs identification to tool implementation.**

## Results

An implementation enables chaining the retrieval of information from a video, processing its transcript, and preparing a Markdown note.

This desktop application was subsequently rewritten in Swift and published on the AppStore (see project)
