# Deploy SAP PM to digitize industrial maintenance across multiple European sites

## Context

Industrial maintenance management requires reliable information on equipment, interventions, and preparation of planned shutdowns.

As part of a European deployment project, the company initiated the go-live of the **SAP Plant Maintenance (SAP PM)** module to integrate these activities into its information system.

This transformation involved resuming existing maintenance data, adapting the new tool to the specificities of installations and business practices, and supporting users in adopting it.

**Objective: Ensure the transition to SAP PM while preserving existing information and enabling maintenance teams to effectively use the new environment.**

## Approach

As **SAP PM Key User**, I served as the business reference for maintenance and shutdown preparation.

My intervention was structured around four axes.

**1. Adapt SAP PM to maintenance needs**

- Analysis of practices and operational needs.
- Identification of specificities related to industrial equipment.
- Consideration of constraints for shutdown preparation and follow-up.
- Definition of necessary adaptations for business operation.
- Interface between field users and the project team.

**2. Support data migration**

The transition to SAP PM required resuming information used in the previous system.

I contributed to the migration of maintenance data to ensure their availability in the new environment.

This step was essential to preserve activity continuity and allow users to retrieve necessary information for their interventions.

**3. Organize workstation deployment**

The deployment of SAP PM also involved making the module accessible to teams in their working environment.

I participated in providing workstations and organizing access to the new tool for concerned interveners.

**4. Train and support users**

The success of the deployment depended on the teams' ability to adopt the new processes.

I provided business support for stakeholders, notably through:

- Presentation of SAP PM functionality.
- Training on key maintenance operations.
- Explanation of practices adapted to the new tool.
- Support during initial adoption.

## My Role

I served as **SAP PM Key User and Maintenance Business Reference** within the framework of the European deployment.

My responsibilities included:

- Representing maintenance requirements to the project team.
- Defining necessary functional adaptations.
- Contributing to the migration of existing data.
- Supporting the rollout of workstations.
- Training users.
- Coordinating between field stakeholders and project actors.

This mission required combining knowledge of maintenance processes, understanding of an industrial ERP, and change management support.

## Results

- **Go-live of the SAP PM module** within the European deployment project.
- Migration of maintenance data to the new environment.
- Provision of workstations for stakeholders.
- Training teams on module usage.
- Incorporation of maintenance specifics and shutdown preparation into functional adaptations.

## Lessons Learned

**Deploying an industrial ERP goes beyond software installation: it requires adapting business processes, preserving data, and supporting users in adopting new practices.**

Three principles emerged from this experience:

- **Start from operational needs**: maintenance processes and equipment constraints must guide system adaptations.
- **Secure information continuity**: data migration is an essential step to enable transition to the new tool.
- **Make users change agents**: training and on-site support are indispensable for adopting new processes.

**This project demonstrates my ability to contribute to the deployment of an industrial information system, bridge the gap between business operations and IT, and support digital transformation across multiple sites.**
