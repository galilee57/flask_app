# Process Quality Support Manager

## Role and period

From 2016 to 2021, I worked as **Process Quality Support Manager**. My role was to maintain process control to achieve the quality objectives of three production lines.

## Risk management

The approach used FMEAs, control plans and feedback from operations. I worked to make this approach practical for operators, incorporate risks introduced by new projects, and use customer feedback to reassess existing risks.

## Operational control

Field work and process monitoring tools such as statistical process control (SPC), standards and checklists helped detect drift and reduce defects. I also led a review and redesign of the operating procedures.

## Process analysis and preventive measures

I led weekly coordination across departments on quality issues. This included tracking KPIs, organising working groups and analysing incidents using 8D, cause trees and Ishikawa diagrams.

## On-call duties

The position included on-call duties and a substantial operational component.
