# Share my experiences and technical knowledge through articles on Medium

## Context

Throughout my professional journey and personal projects, I have developed skills in several areas: industrial engineering, Python programming, Big Data, web development, and artificial intelligence.

These experiences allowed me to address numerous technical challenges, discover new tools, and gradually build my own working methods.

I wished to extend this learning approach with a project for writing and publishing articles on **Medium**.

My ambition is to transform some of my experiments into structured, understandable, and useful content for others interested in these technologies.

This project also serves as a means to take a step back from my achievements and better formalize the acquired knowledge.

## Objectives

- Develop a technical writing practice.
- Share acquired knowledge through my projects.
- Structure and deepen my understanding of the topics addressed.
- Make accessible sometimes complex issues.
- Complete my portfolio with more in-depth content.
- Gradually build an editorial presence on Medium.

## Approach and Editorial Project

### 1. Identify Topics to Share

I wish to rely on my professional and personal projects to identify topics likely to give rise to articles.

Several areas of my career constitute editorial leads:

- Python development and software architectures.
- Big Data and data processing with PySpark.
- AI agents and language models.
- Development assisted by OpenAI Codex.
- Web application design.

### 2. Transform an Experiment into an Article

One of the challenges of this approach is to go beyond the simple presentation of a project.

A technical article must allow the reader to understand the problem, the context, design choices, difficulties encountered, results, and lessons learned

### 3. Develop a Pedagogical Approach

I wish to prioritize articles based on concrete situations and understandable examples.

The goal is to make technical topics accessible without losing their precision.

Depending on the themes, articles could include code snippets, architecture diagrams, illustrations, and case studies.

This approach aligns with my interest in knowledge transfer and supporting individuals in discovering new tools.

### 4. Articulating Medium and my personal portfolio

My portfolio serves as a concise presentation space for my achievements.

Medium complements it by offering more detailed content on certain experiments.

### 5. Gradually building a publication method

The editorial project also requires defining a personal organization to move from an idea to a publishable article.

## My Role

**I initiated this personal publication project on Medium to share my technical experiences and formalize the knowledge acquired throughout my career.**

## Current Status and Results

6 articles published on Medium.

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Popularization requires rigor

Making a topic accessible does not mean oversimplifying it. One must find a balance between technical precision, clarity, and pedagogical progression.

### 2. Sharing is part of learning

Sharing knowledge represents a new step in mastering a subject and encourages continuing experiments.

**This project reflects my willingness to extend my technical achievements through a transmission approach, using writing to structure my knowledge and share my experiences with a wider community.**
