Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: horizontal portfolio project cover, landscape 3:2. Create a refined editorial 3D illustration with tactile paper and matte materials, off-white background, deep burgundy accents and pale green surfaces, neutral graphite details, generous breathing room, clear legibility at thumbnail size. No recognizable people, place names, real masthead, fake readable article, or names. Convey interviewing, photographing, writing and reviewing proofs.
