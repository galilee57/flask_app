## The project

Develop a desktop application to design, execute, and supervise multi-agent processes by combining Ollama and OpenAI, then apply it to the document analysis of health data.

## My contribution

**I designed and led the development of this multi-agent orchestration platform in Rust, with assistance from OpenAI Codex.**

I personally defined expected use cases, interface features, and process organization principles.

## Results

The project resulted in the development of a native Rust application.
