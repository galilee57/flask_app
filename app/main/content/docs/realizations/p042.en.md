# Develop the Data and AI culture and support tool adoption

## About the Project

The democratization of Data, Big Data, and Artificial Intelligence technologies is transforming the possibilities for data analysis and operations in industry.

However, their adoption remains often hindered by the perceived complexity of tools, working habits, and the difficulty in identifying concrete applications.

Business teams have access to numerous data sources, but their exploitation still frequently relies on manual processing, Excel files, or individual practices that are difficult to reproduce.

**Goal: Make Data and AI technologies accessible, develop employees' skills, and promote the adoption of more reliable, reproducible, and automatable analysis methods.**

## Approach

I lead a Data and AI community by prioritizing a progressive, concrete, and business-need-centered approach.

This approach is based on four complementary pillars.

**1. Raise awareness and demystify**

Animation of *Coffee Breaks Data & AI* to present technologies, explain their capabilities, and share examples of industrial applications.

These exchanges allow technical concepts to be addressed in an accessible format and spark employees' curiosity.

**2. Train through practice**

Organization and contribution to workshops, practical sessions, and roadshows to support teams in discovering and using tools.

Demonstrations are based on business use cases to directly link learning to daily challenges encountered.

**3. Foster autonomy**

Promotion and support for the use of KNIME and Data tools adapted to users' needs.

Emphasis is placed on acquiring transferable skills:

- Data structuring and preparation.
- Joins and transformations.
- Quality and consistency checks.
- Automation of processing.
- Reproducibility of analyses.
- Exploitation of data available on Big Data platforms.

**4. Federate and Capitalize**

Sharing best practices, lessons learned, and team achievements.

The objective is to foster exchanges between users, highlight initiatives, and build a community of mutual support around Data and AI usage.

## Achievements

**Animation and Communication**

Preparation and animation of Data and AI awareness sessions, with simplification of concepts and presentation of use cases.

**Tool Support**

Demonstrations and support for using KNIME, data analysis tools, and Big Data resources.

**Workshops and Business Proximity**

Participation in workshops and roadshows to meet with teams and facilitate the adoption of technologies.

**Knowledge Capitalization**

Contribution to the creation and sharing of materials - via SharePoint, methods, and best practices to extend learning beyond sessions.

## My Role

I contributed to bridging Data technical skills with the operational needs of business teams.

My intervention focused on:

- Simplifying Data, Big Data, and AI concepts.
- Animating awareness sessions and exchanges.
- Providing practical support to users.
- Promoting reproducible analysis methods.
- Sharing knowledge and lessons learned.
- Developing a collective dynamic around Data usage.

This approach leverages both my technical skills, my understanding of industrial challenges, and my ability to communicate.

## Results

- Implementation and animation of awareness and sharing formats around Data and AI.
- The usage rate of KNIME and Big Data environments has increased continuously.
- Highlighting industrial use cases to make technologies more concrete.
- Contributing to the development of exchanges and knowledge sharing between business users and Data specialists.

## Lessons Learned

**Data transformation does not rely solely on technology availability: it depends on teams' ability to adopt these tools.**

**The challenge is to evolve practices: moving from occasional use of Data tools to a culture of analysis, automation, and knowledge sharing.**

> A often overlooked challenge is fostering a COMMON LANGUAGE that allows all business functions to understand each other. The adoption of tools structures and determines the *LINGUA FRANCA* naturally.
