# Animation of quality risk control · FMEA

## Situation
In an industrial environment, quality risk control does not rely on a static document. Risk analyses must evolve based on process changes, incidents, non-conformities, and lessons learned.

FMEA is a tool that must be regularly revised and kept up to date, with traceability of modifications. This logic aims to maintain consistency between identified risks and the reality of the process.

The system does not limit itself to FMEA. It fits into a chain linking non-conformity analyses, action plans, monitoring plans, standards, and degraded operating situations.

## Challenge

The challenge is therefore not simply to write an FMEA, but to maintain quality documentation consistent with industrial reality and lessons learned.

Beyond the regulatory aspect, it is a whole animation approach that I implemented around FMEA control: moving teams from concepts to real operational use.

The redesign of degraded steps is an example of aligning operational tools with FMEAs.

## Approach

A first component of the work consisted of formalizing certain interactions between quality events, process changes, FMEA, and monitoring plan.

A SIPOC allows explicitly positioning non-conformities, failures, analyses, disputes, process modifications, and reverse FMEAs among the elements intervening in the FMEA modification process and its action plans.

This experience subsequently allowed me to develop an application exploiting FMEA extractions to propose different representations of the FMEA and Control Plan.

This solution received feedback regarding filters, special characteristics, responsible persons, and measuring equipment.

## My Role

As Process & Quality Manager, drafting and distributing FMEAs down to operators.
As Support Manager, broader reflection using SIPOCs to help the organization reposition each person's role around FMEAs.

## Results

Better adoption by teams and organizations of FMEAs as a central tool:

- reduction in non-conformities;
- improvement in 8D closure times;
- alignment with degraded steps and actions to protect the customer at the station in case of deviation

## Lessons Learned

Mastery of FMEA (AMDEC) does not rely solely on its initial drafting.

> Its operational value also depends on its maintenance, its re-closure with field events, and the ability of business units to exploit the information it contains.
>
