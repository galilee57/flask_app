# Develop an understanding of human relationships through university studies in psychology

## Context

In parallel with my technical and professional career, I undertook university studies in psychology.

I followed this curriculum at a distance, within the framework of training associated with CNED and Paris VIII University, and obtained a **psychology degree in 2007**.

This approach responded to my interest in understanding human behaviors, interpersonal relationships, and the mechanisms that influence interactions within groups.

It also allowed me to approach family and professional relationships from a different angle than my engineering training.

My career was completed by an internship at a Public Mental Health Establishment (EPSM).

## Objectives

- Obtain university training in psychology.
- Deepen my understanding of human functioning.
- Study interpersonal relationships and group dynamics.
- Discover the approaches and methods of psychology.
- Confront university teachings with a specialized professional environment.
- Enrich my understanding of family and professional relationships.
- Develop an approach to management attentive to human dimensions.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Follow a university curriculum in psychology

I undertook psychology studies within the framework of a distance learning curriculum associated with Paris VIII University and CNED.

This training led me to develop university knowledge in a discipline different from my initial technical field.

I pursued this path until obtaining my degree in 2007.

This approach testifies to my willingness to broaden my knowledge and pursue demanding learning beyond my professional specialty.

### 2. Deepen the understanding of human relationships

My studies allowed me to discover different approaches to psychology and reflect on the mechanisms involved in behaviors and relationships between individuals.

I paid particular attention to understanding human relationships, particularly in family and professional environments.

This knowledge provided a complementary perspective for grasping interactions, perceptual differences, and relational situations.

### 3. Complete an internship at a mental health facility

As part of my career path, I completed an internship at a Public Mental Health Establishment (EPSM).

This experience allowed me to discover a professional environment dedicated to mental health and to confront certain university teachings with the reality of a specialized establishment.

The reception service, missions undertaken, and observed activities can be specified to better document this experience.

This internship constitutes a training and professional discovery experience, without being presented as an autonomous activity of a psychologist.

### 4. Connect teachings with my professional relationships

I gradually established links between the knowledge acquired in psychology and the situations encountered in my professional life.

This training provided me with reference points to reflect on several important dimensions of management:

- Interpersonal communication.
- Understanding behaviors.
- Relationships within teams.
- Differences in perception.
- Tensions and disagreement situations.
- Taking into account the human dimension in decisions.

It thus complements my technical and organizational approach to industrial projects.

### 5. Enrich my reflection on family relationships

Beyond the professional context, these studies also nourished my reflection on family relationships and daily interactions.

They allowed me to approach these subjects with university knowledge and particular attention to the complexity of human relationships.

## My Role

**I personally undertook and completed a university curriculum in psychology, obtained my license in 2007, and completed an internship at an EPSM.**

I then applied the knowledge from this training in my reflection on human relationships, particularly in family and professional contexts.

This approach constitutes a personal investment in a discipline complementary to engineering.

## Training and Skills

**Diploma:** Psychology License — University Paris VIII, obtained in 2007.

**Mode:** distance learning curriculum associated with CNED.

**Experience:** internship at a Public Mental Health Establishment.

**Knowledge Areas:** psychology, human behaviors, interpersonal relationships, group dynamics.

**Transferable Skills:** listening, analysis of relational situations, communication, stepping back, understanding the human dimensions of management.

## Results

- Obtaining a university degree in psychology in 2007.
- Acquiring knowledge in a complementary discipline to engineering.
- Completing an internship at a mental health facility.
- Developing reflection on family and professional relationships.
- Enriching my personal approach to management.

This university training is not presented as a qualification for autonomous clinical practice in psychology.

## Teaching

### 1. The human dimension is essential to management

Projects and organizations do not rely solely on processes, technical means, or indicators.

They mobilize people, behaviors, and relationships that influence their functioning.

### 2. Understanding behaviors allows for stepping back

Knowledge in psychology provides useful perspectives for analyzing a relational situation beyond its apparent dimension alone.

### 3. Technical and human skills are complementary

Engineering enables addressing systems, processes, and organizations; psychology offers another perspective on interactions between people.

### 4. Distance learning fosters autonomy

Completing a university curriculum within this framework requires organization, perseverance, and sustained personal commitment.

### 5. Learning another discipline enriches professional practice

Studying psychology allowed me to move beyond an exclusively technical approach and integrate the relational dimension more fully into my reflection on management.

**Obtaining my psychology degree in 2007 constitutes an important milestone in my career. It complements my engineering training and nurtures a management approach that combines technical understanding, organization, and attention to human relationships.**
