## Education

I earned a bachelor’s degree in psychology from Paris VIII University in 2007 through distance learning. My transcript covers cognitive, developmental, social and clinical psychology, alongside ergonomics and neuropsychology.

The programme combined survey and interviewing methods with descriptive and inferential statistics. It also included supervised projects and a **100-hour placement** at a public mental health facility.

## My contribution

I then applied the knowledge from this training in my reflection on human relationships, particularly in family and professional contexts.

This approach constitutes a personal investment in a discipline complementary to engineering.

## Results

- Acquiring knowledge in a complementary discipline to engineering.

- Completing an internship at a mental health facility.
