# Process Quality Support Manager

## Role

From 2016 to 2021, I worked as **Process Quality Support Manager**, overseeing process control to meet quality objectives across three production lines.

## Scope

I worked on FMEAs, control plans and customer feedback, and on shop-floor monitoring through SPC, standards and checklists. I also led the review of operating procedures and coordinated weekly quality work using KPIs, working groups, 8D, cause trees and Ishikawa diagrams. The position included on-call duties.
