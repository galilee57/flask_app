# Arts et Métiers engineering degree (ENSAM)

## Education

From 1993 to 1996, I completed the Arts et Métiers generalist engineering programme (ENSAM). The curriculum covers structural and fluid mechanics, materials and energy engineering, engineering design, manufacturing processes, industrial methods and production organisation. It also includes electrical engineering, control, computing, wood processing, communication and management.

## Final-year project

My final-year project focused on **very-high-speed bearings** for aviation applications.

## Career context

I have drawn on this foundation in technical acceptance of rotating machinery, rolling-pass simulation, thermal efficiency improvements, industrial methods and process engineering.
