# Deploy and qualify an optical measurement system to monitor product cleanliness

## About this project

On a metal strip processing industrial line, surface cleanliness is an essential parameter to control product quality.

Spot checks did not allow for a continuous view of the cleanliness status of the strips.

To strengthen process monitoring, we introduced an optical device based on measuring the reflectivity of the metal surface.

**I participated in installing the device, its technical qualification, and defining its calibration method, then contributed to operating its data with Power BI.**

## Objectives

- Have a continuous measurement of surface cleanliness.
- Identify process drift situations.
- Determine the sensor's performance and limits.
- Define a calibration method adapted to industrial conditions.
- Make data accessible and usable for process teams.
- Ensure the sustainable use of the device in line monitoring.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Install the optical measurement device

I worked with a specialist to install the optical system on the industrial line.

The device was based on analyzing the reflectivity of the metal strip to obtain a surface cleanliness indicator.

The goal was to have continuous information directly from the process.

### 2. Study the sensor's performance

I participated in studying the device's behavior to determine under what conditions its measurements could be used.

The qualification work highlighted its ability to distinguish different cleanliness situations.

**We thus characterized the device as a process monitoring indicator, and not as a universal absolute measurement of dirt quantity.**

### 3. Define the calibration method

To ensure sustainable use of the device, I worked with the specialist on defining a calibration method.

This step was essential to have a verification and adjustment method for the measurement.

It also facilitated subsequent interventions by instrumentation teams.

### 4. Exploit data with Power BI

I contributed to exploiting VisioClean data using Power BI.

The objective was to transform device measurements into useful information for process monitoring.

### 5. Integrate measurement into industrial monitoring

Qualification work clarified the conditions for using the signal in degreasing monitoring.

The optical measurement could contribute to detecting certain abnormal situations, without automatically identifying their cause.

Its use therefore had to be associated with knowledge of the process and, if necessary, additional checks.

The device was subsequently integrated into industrial monitoring practices.

Its cleaning, particularly that of the lens, is included in maintenance operations, and its signal is taken into account in monitoring and drift reaction documents.

## My Role

**I contributed to the implementation and qualification of the device, intervening at the interface between instrumentation, metrology, data analysis, and process control.**

The project was carried out collectively with instrumentation, maintenance, and process teams.

## Results

### Provision of a continuous measurement

The device enabled having an indicator of surface cleanliness before and after degreasing.

### Qualification of capabilities and limits

Studies confirmed the system's ability to differentiate certain cleanliness situations, while identifying precision limits and the influence of product families.

### Implementation of calibration means

Reflectivity standards were acquired and a specific support was manufactured to enable calibration operations in the workshop.

### Data valorization

Measurements were exploited in Power BI to facilitate their analysis and use for process monitoring.

### Industrial sustainability

The device remained integrated into the line's monitoring and maintenance practices.

## Lessons Learned

### Metrology determines long-term reliability

Defining a calibration method and having access to appropriate standards are essential for ensuring the sustainable use of an industrial device.

### A process indicator is not necessarily an absolute measurement

A measurement can be relevant for detecting drift without allowing perfect quantification of a physical phenomenon.

**This project demonstrates my ability to contribute to the deployment of a new measurement technology, qualify its performance, and transform its data into actionable information for process control.**
