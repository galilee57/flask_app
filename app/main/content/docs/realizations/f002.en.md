# Specialized Mastère in Maintenance Management — Arts et Métiers (ENSAM)

## Background

I completed the Specialized Mastère in Maintenance Management at Arts et Métiers (ENSAM) to deepen my understanding of maintenance management after my engineering degree. The programme connected equipment diagnostics with decisions about availability, costs and team organisation.

## Programme structure

The one-year programme combined:

- **380 hours of instruction**, including classes, case studies and site visits.
- An **industrial study lasting more than six months in a company** that culminated in a **professional thesis defended before a panel**.

## Maintenance management — 155 hours

- Concepts and methodology
- Dependability
- Economic and financial analysis
- Inventory management
- Quality and maintenance
- Maintenance procurement and contracts

## Maintenance techniques and operations — 225 hours

- Maintenance engineering
- Component characteristics and behaviour
- Maintenance management
- Decision-support systems
- Maintenance policy
- Human factors
- Understanding the business environment

## Industrial study and thesis defence

The study began alongside classes and later continued full-time in the company. It was supervised by an industry project lead and an academic adviser. The study led to a **professional thesis**, defended at the end of the programme.

## Professional thesis — Preventive maintenance plan at Saint-Fons Chimie

My thesis focused on **setting up a preventive maintenance plan for critical machinery at the Rhône-Poulenc Saint-Fons Chimie site in the Rhône region of France**. It addressed economic questions about costs and investment, technical questions about equipment monitoring, and organisational questions about training and coordination.

### Review existing practices and identify critical machinery

I reviewed the practices already in place: vibration analysis, inspection rounds and oil analysis. I used a **simplified failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)** to select the equipment to prioritise in the plan.

### Build a team and train it in vibration measurement

The work involved site staff and external specialists, including Bently Nevada, Brüel & Kjær and Campagna. It included building a team trained in preventive maintenance.

I set up training on a **physical test rig that reproduced mechanical faults**. The team practised taking vibration measurements and interpreting signals from known faults.

### Propose investments

The thesis set out investment proposals to support implementation of the preventive maintenance plan.

## Career context

This programme was a foundation for my career, giving me experience and methods I applied in several subsequent roles. The entries on regional maintenance management, equipment reliability and maintenance cost planning illustrate how I put those teachings into practice.
