## The project

My first app published on the App Store, developed with Codex

## My contribution

I identified the need, defined the scope of the application, and led the project to its publication. I relied on **Codex as a development assistant**, while retaining responsibility for product choices and the published version.

This experience gave me two roles on the same project. As a user, I could formulate the problem based on a concrete situation. As the project owner, I had to transform this expectation into a precise enough path to guide development and bring it to completion.

## Results

**YouTube to Obsidian is my first app published on the App Store.** The project resulted in an application available publicly and gave me my first experience of the iOS product realization cycle, from need definition to distribution.
