# From Observation to Planetary Images

<!-- media: saturne ; emplacement: ouverture -->

<!-- section: apropos -->

## Moving from observation to production

Long-time astronomy enthusiast, this project allowed me to extend my sky observation practice by taking my own photographs.
I worked on several elements of the same set: the telescope, the mount, the camera, and the capture and processing software.

### Several constraints
Light pollution, heat disturbances, equipment transport and power supply. Mounting and tracking adjustments take up a significant place in each session.

### My Role
Equipment selection, replacing the mount transmission with a pulley kit generating less vibration, image acquisition and processing.

## Equipment & Software

### Observe & Track

- Maksutov Skywatcher 180/2700
- HEQ-5 Pro SynScan GoTo Mount

### Adapt the Transmission

- Rowan Astro Kit, pulleys and belts

### Acquire & Process

- ZWO ASI224MC Camera
- FireCapture (video capture)
- Siril (video processing and image generation)

<!-- section: architecture -->

## One Chain, from Sky to Image

Each component ensures a function in the production of the photograph.

### 01 — PREPARE
Optics & Mount Observe, mount and ensure tracking during acquisitions.
### 02 — CAPTURE
Camera & FireCapture Record videos of the planet with the camera.
### 03 — PROCESS
Siril Recrop by object tracking, work on sharpness and combine images.
### 04 — PRODUCE
Final Image Keep a photograph resulting from capture and processing.

<!-- section: focus -->

## The Choices Behind Production

### Plan for Evolution Possibilities

Before buying, I researched adaptation possibilities. I notably compared kits allowing the evolution of the mount and optical support.
Scalability was part of my approach from equipment selection.

### Replace the Transmission

I wanted to achieve finer control over tracking and limit vibrations associated with the gears. I dismantled the old transmission, then installed a Rowan kit with pulleys and belts.

*Reducing vibrations allowed for sharper images.*

<!-- media: transmission-photo -->

### From film to image production

Final images are obtained from the camera stream in FireCapture. Processing is done with Siril: automatic reframing via object tracking, balancing settings for optimal sharpness, combining images to produce the final photograph.

<!-- section: enseignement -->

## Lessons Learned

> "The best equipment is the equipment you use."

Indeed, portable and easy-to-deploy equipment should be prioritized, as conditions can be challenging (cold, rain, time constraints).
Technology evolves rapidly: Wi-Fi control became widespread in 2016. Alignment has become reproducible and easier.
The quality of the equipment and its modularity (the mount's ability to accept optics from different manufacturers) are essential.
