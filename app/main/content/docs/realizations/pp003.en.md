# Exploring digital manufacturing and prototyping in a fablab

## About this project

In a personal approach to developing my technical skills, I undertook training in a fablab to discover the possibilities offered by digital manufacturing tools.

The objective was to understand how to go from digital design to the realization of physical objects, using equipment accessible in a prototyping workshop.

This experience focused on 3D printing, laser cutting, and a drone fabrication project.

## Objectives

- Discover the principles of digital manufacturing.
- Learn to use fablab equipment.
- Experiment with different prototyping techniques.
- Understand the preparation of files for machines.
- Create physical objects from digital models.
- Explore the fabrication of a more complex technical system through a drone project.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Training on digital manufacturing tools

I undertook practical training in a fablab to discover the equipment, their capabilities, and their usage constraints.

### 2. Experimenting with laser cutting

I explored the fabrication of objects from vector files intended for laser cutting.

This technique allows transforming digital contours into physical parts, notably to create shapes, assemblies, and customized objects.


### 3. Discovering 3D printing

I also experimented with 3D printing to discover the possibilities of additive manufacturing.


### 4. Participating in a drone fabrication project

This project represented a more complex application of the digital manufacturing approach, involving an object combining several technical components.

It allowed me to explore the realization of a system going beyond the fabrication of an isolated part.

## My Role

**I undertook this training and experimentation process to develop my skills in digital manufacturing and discover the possibilities of prototyping in a fablab.**

These activities were carried out in a learning environment with support.

## Technologies and methods

**Technologies:** 3D printing, laser cutting, SVG vector files, fablab equipment.

**Methods:** digital manufacturing, prototyping, hands-on learning, experimentation, creation of physical objects.

## Results

- Practical training in digital manufacturing techniques.
- Creation of objects using laser cutting and 3D printing.
- Manufacturing of a drone and testing at Drone Parc

## Lessons learned

### Develop a multidisciplinary approach

A fablab fosters exchanges between design, mechanics, manufacturing, and, depending on the projects, electronics and programming.

### Strengthen a prototyping culture

This experience complements my industrial and digital skills by developing a concrete approach to manufacturing and experimentation.

**This project illustrates my technological curiosity and my willingness to develop practical skills in digital manufacturing, exploring tools that enable transforming digital models into physical realizations.**
