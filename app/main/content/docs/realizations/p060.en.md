# Lead a chemical cleaning project on an industrial facility

## About the Project

Chemical cleaning must be carried out during a scheduled shutdown, in interaction with other interventions.

Cleaning requires a specialized company, with its own temporary equipment, chemical products, and connection needs to existing installations.

The project also takes into account effluent management and a solution for temporary storage of neutralized water.

**I took charge of this project by leading the subcontractor responsible for chemical cleaning, from the technical preparation of the intervention to its execution.**

## Objectives

The challenge was to enable the execution of a specialized operation under conditions compatible with the constraints of the facility and the industrial shutdown.

## Approach

### 1. Technically prepare the intervention

I contacted the specialized company to define the conditions for carrying out the cleaning and transmit the necessary information for preparing its offer.

I specifically clarified:

- The characteristics of the circuits to be cleaned.
- The nature of certain piping.
- Connection possibilities.
- Available electrical supplies.
- Compressed air supply possibilities.
- The absence of available steam.
- Access conditions to the intervention zone.

This preparation allowed the provider to size its resources and anticipate necessary adaptations for its temporary installation.

### 2. Lead the specialized subcontractor

I ensured the leadership of the contractor within the framework of this operation.

My role consisted of bridging the gap between the facility's requirements and the technical resources mobilized by the external company.

It also required taking into account the constraints of a project carried out during a shutdown, with other scheduled interventions nearby.

### 3. Anticipate the management of chemical products and effluents

The nature of the operation required particular attention to the products used and the effluents generated.

A specific constraint concerned the wastewater network, which was unavailable for part of the shutdown due to other works.

### 4. Coordinate interfaces with other stakeholders

The project was part of a shutdown program involving several simultaneous operations.

Preparation therefore had to integrate interactions with other works, particularly those affecting networks and utilities.

### 5. Carry out cleaning and take into account lessons learned

The chemical cleaning operation was carried out.

Lessons learned highlight the importance of not limiting the evaluation of an intervention to its execution, but also taking into account the behavior of the installation after restart.

## My Role

**I took charge of organizing and managing the chemical cleaning project, ensuring the interface with the specialized subcontractor.**

This mission mobilized both my technical knowledge of the installation and my skills in managing external companies.

## Results

**A specialized chemical cleaning project prepared, coordinated, and carried out during an industrial shutdown.**

Reduction of defects related to particle entrainment in subsequent cycles. The cleaning frequency could be adapted accordingly.

## Lessons Learned

**The success of a specialized industrial project relies on anticipating technical interfaces and on the quality of managing external stakeholders.**

**This project demonstrates my ability to take charge of a complex technical industrial project, manage a specialized contractor, and coordinate an intervention under safety, environmental, and operations constraints.**
