# Lead and generalize the technology for improving the thermal efficiency of an industrial furnace

## Context

As part of the effort to enhance the energy performance of an industrial furnace, we studied a technology involving inserts designed to modify heat exchange and reduce the temperature of recuperators.

Feedback from other installations was mixed. Before committing to an investment across the entire furnace, it was necessary to verify the performance of this technology under our own production conditions.

**I took charge of the technical scoping of the experiment, the industrial benchmarking, and the performance analysis to inform the decision on generalization.**

## Objectives

- Verify the thermal efficiency of the inserts.
- Reduce the temperature of the recuperators.
- Evaluate the impact on energy consumption.
- Verify the behavior of the technology under industrial conditions.
- Have reliable results before large-scale deployment.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Structure the Experiment

I developed the initial action plan and organized the approach.

I personally solicited several industrial sites to gather their feedback on temperatures, energy consumption, mechanical integrity of equipment, and difficulties encountered.

These exchanges confirmed the interest of validating the technology on our own installation.

### 2. Organize a Test on a Reduced Number of Tubes

The protocol was based on a comparison between equipped and unequipped tubes, complemented by an analysis before and after modification.

I contributed to defining the process parameters to be monitored in order to obtain usable measurements.

### 3. Analyze Thermal Performance

I performed data extraction and compared temperatures across different configurations.

The analysis confirmed the expected thermal behavior.

I synthesized the results in a technical presentation intended to share observations and prepare subsequent decisions.

### 4. Generalize the technology in the following year

Following the results obtained and the demonstration of the gain, the solution was generalized in the following year to other radiant tubes of the furnace.

This approach allowed limiting the initial risk before committing to a larger deployment.

## My Role

I personally undertook:

- Structuring the action plan.
- Inter-site technical benchmarking.
- Contribution to the experimental protocol.
- Drafting and presenting technical results.

Material interventions, instrumentation, and generalization mobilized the installation's technical teams.

## Results

**Confirmed thermal effect:** reduction of temperatures at the recuperators.

**Demonstrated gain:** continuing the evaluation allowed confirming the interest of the technology.

The project thus went beyond the experimental stage to lead to an effective modification of the installation.

## Skills applied

- Industrial thermal engineering.
- Design of test protocols.
- Instrumentation and data acquisition.
- Statistical analysis and performance comparison.
- Techno-economic evaluation.
- Supporting an investment decision.

## Lessons Learned

This project illustrates the interest of a progressive experimental approach to evaluate an industrial innovation.

The trial on a few tubes allowed confirming the sought-after thermal mechanism and preparing a more complete evaluation of the energy gain.

The subsequent demonstration of the gain led to the generalization of the technology.

**This achievement demonstrates my ability to structure an industrial experiment, analyze its performance, and contribute to transforming a technical innovation into concrete improvement of a production facility.**
