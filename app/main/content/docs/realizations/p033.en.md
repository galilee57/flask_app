# Manage a crisis following a major furnace failure

## About the project

A major incident occurred on an industrial furnace: **the fall of refractory elements caused production blockage**.

This situation required urgent intervention.

The repair had to take into account several simultaneous issues:

- Understanding the failure and its consequences on the facilities.
- Safety of intervenors in a degraded industrial environment.
- Identification of necessary work.
- Availability of technical means and spare parts.
- Coordination of operations in an emergency context.
- Preparation of a restart under controlled conditions.

**Objective: organize the technical response to a major furnace failure to restore the conditions necessary for resuming production, without compromising safety or facility reliability.**

## Approach

### 1. Face an industrial crisis situation

The fall of refractories led to a production stoppage requiring rapid mobilization of technical skills.

In this context, the priority was to characterize the consequences of the incident, identify associated risks, and determine the conditions necessary for intervention.

Managing such a situation required balancing industrial urgency with the rigor indispensable for work on critical equipment.

### 2. Structure the technical diagnosis

Technical exchanges regarding the furnace notably concerned product conveyance difficulties.

An inspection reported no visible breakage on the examined elements, while raising the hypothesis of wear on certain components.

### 3. Prepare repair interventions

Repairing an industrial furnace after a major failure requires articulating work on damaged elements with checks on associated equipment.

Investigations had to allow defining the scope of repairs, necessary components, and preliminary checks before resumption.

The issue was to prepare a technically justified intervention, while taking into account the pressure exerted by the production stoppage.

### 4. Coordinate technical exchanges

In a crisis context, this coordination is an essential element to advance the diagnosis and prepare intervention decisions.

## My Role

I led the coordination around the incident affecting the furnace.

My involvement was within a context of exceptional maintenance, with an immediate stake in production continuity.

## Results

Control of intervention conditions: safety, costs, quality

## Lessons Learned

**A major failure of an industrial equipment requires managing simultaneously the urgency of production, the rigor of the diagnosis, and the safety of interventions.**

**This project illustrates my involvement in managing a major industrial failure, at the intersection of mechanical diagnosis, exceptional maintenance, and production continuity imperatives.**
