# Arts et Métiers engineering degree (ENSAM)

## Background

From 1993 to 1996, I completed the Arts et Métiers generalist engineering programme (ENSAM). The curriculum covers the full range of industrial engineering, from studying systems to designing and manufacturing them and organising production.

## Mechanics, materials and energy

- Continuum mechanics
- Elements of structural mechanics
- Mechanics of deformable solids
- Mechanical system dynamics
- Materials
- Fluid mechanics
- Heat transfer
- Theory of machines
- Energy engineering
- Energy engineering and steam machinery

## Design, manufacturing processes and production

- Engineering design and design office practice
- Machining techniques
- Materials and process interactions
- Computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) for material removal
- Forming and permanent joining processes
- Foundry processes
- Industrial methods
- Production organisation

## Electrical engineering, control and computing

- Electrical engineering
- Power electronics
- Electronics
- Systems analysis
- Continuous-time control
- Sequential control
- Computer science
- Algorithms and programming
- Mathematics for decision-making and signal processing
- Numerical algorithms

## Wood processing

- Specialist wood processing techniques
- Instrumentation in the wood industries
- Implementation of wood forming processes

## Communication, economics and management

- Methodology and communication
- General economics
- Marketing
- Financial and cost accounting
- Company law

## Final-year project

My final-year project focused on **very-high-speed bearings** for aviation applications.

## Career context

Mechanical systems, materials, manufacturing processes and heat transfer have been part of my work throughout my career, from technical acceptance of rotating machinery, rolling-pass simulation and thermal efficiency improvements to industrial methods and process engineering.
