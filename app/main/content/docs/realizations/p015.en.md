# Lead industrial maintenance projects under safety and schedule constraints

## About this project

Heat exchangers play an essential role in the operation of industrial installations. Their fouling can degrade heat exchange performance and require periodic cleaning operations.

As part of a maintenance intervention, I ensured the **leadership of a heat exchanger cleaning project**, carried out by a subcontractor company.

The project presented three major constraints:

- **Safety**: high-pressure cleaning operations required a secured work zone and prohibited any concurrent activities.
- **Schedule**: interventions had to be performed during hidden time windows, without disrupting other planned operations.
- **Technical**: cleaning could reveal sealing defects requiring additional repairs before equipment return to service.

**Objective: complete heat exchanger cleaning within the allotted deadlines, ensuring personnel safety and integrating any necessary repair work.**

## Approach

I organized and coordinated the project around four axes.

**1. Prepare the intervention**

- Definition of the cleaning operations scope.
- Coordination with the subcontractor company.
- Organization of access routes and the intervention zone.
- Integration of safety constraints related to high-pressure cleaning.
- Scheduling work in coherence with other industrial activities.

**2. Control risks associated with high pressure**

High-pressure cleaning constituted a major risk requiring specific measures.

The project organization was based on:

- Delimitation of a dedicated work zone.
- Prohibition of concurrent activities during cleaning operations.
- Coordination of access with other stakeholders.
- Compliance with safety conditions applicable to high-pressure work.

Safety was integrated from the project preparation phase and taken into account in its operational organization.

**3. Optimize scheduling during hidden time windows**

Cleaning operations had to fit within a constrained intervention window.

I coordinated the sequencing of activities with the subcontractor to minimize waiting times and respect the overall schedule.

This organization required anticipating interfaces between different operations and reacting quickly to technical uncertainties.

**4. Manage Unplanned Repairs**

Cleaning could reveal leaks on certain tubes of the heat exchangers.

These situations required additional interventions, including:

- **Tube sealing**, intended to restore tightness at the assembly level of a tube.
- **Isolation of a defective tube**, when its restoration was not retained.

I integrated these uncertainties into site monitoring and coordinated their management to preserve project deadlines.

## My Role

I ensured **operational management of the project**, from organizing the intervention to monitoring its execution.

My responsibilities covered:

- Coordination of the subcontractor company.
- Organization of the work area.
- Taking into account safety and coactivity constraints.
- Planning operations in hidden time (time masqué).
- Monitoring progress of works.
- Management of technical uncertainties and additional repairs.
- Compliance with project deadlines.

This project led me to align safety requirements, technical constraints, and facility availability imperatives.

## Results

- **Cleaning of heat exchangers completed within scheduled deadlines.**
- Organization of an intervention area adapted to high-pressure cleaning constraints.
- Management of the prohibition of coactivity during operations.
- Integration of repair uncertainties into project execution.
- Coordination of cleaning operations and additional interventions without exceeding the overall deadline.

## Lessons Learned

**The success of an industrial project relies on the ability to anticipate risks, coordinate stakeholders, and manage uncertainties while respecting operational constraints.**

Three principles emerge from this experience:

- **Integrate safety from the planning stage**: constraints related to hazardous works determine site organization, access points, and coactivity possibilities.
- **Master interfaces**: coordination of subcontractors and sequencing of operations are critical when works must be performed in hidden time.
- **Allow reaction margins for uncertainties**: a cleaning intervention may reveal repair needs that must be integrated quickly without compromising the schedule.

**This project illustrates my ability to manage a concrete industrial intervention, balancing personnel safety, technical coordination, and adherence to deadlines.**
