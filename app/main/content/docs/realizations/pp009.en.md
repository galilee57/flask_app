# Exploring Image Technologies: Aerial Photography, 360° Video, and Virtual Reality

## Context

Passionate about digital technologies and their creative applications, I have developed several practices around photography and video.

I notably explored Insta360 cameras, aerial photography with drones, and shooting using a kite.

These experiments led me to become interested in the entire audiovisual production chain: image acquisition, processing, editing, and delivery.

I also discovered the possibilities of immersive video through the use of a Meta Quest virtual reality headset.

These activities form the starting point of my YouTube channel project.

## Objectives

- Experiment with different photography and video techniques.
- Explore the capture possibilities offered by a 360° camera.
- Shoot from unusual viewpoints.
- Develop my editing and post-production skills.
- Understand the particularities of immersive content.
- Discover the delivery of 360° videos in virtual reality.
- Prepare the promotion of my audiovisual works on YouTube.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Exploring Shooting with Insta360

I used an Insta360 camera to experiment with 360° image capture.

This technology allows recording the environment in all directions and exploring different framings from a single shot.

It opens new creative possibilities, particularly for creating immersive sequences and selecting viewpoints during editing.

This experience led me to discover the particularities of panoramic images and 360° video formats.

### 2. Experimenting with Kite Shooting

I explored an original capture technique using a kite with an Insta360 camera.

This approach allows experimenting with an aerial viewpoint using a device different from a drone.

It involves several technical dimensions:

- Camera positioning.
- Constraints related to the recording medium.
- Image orientation and framing.
- Retrieval and operations on sequences.

This experience illustrates my interest in unconventional recording solutions.

### 3. Practicing aerial photography with drones

I also used a drone to capture aerial footage.

This technique offers a different perspective from ground-level photography and allows exploring image composition from an elevated viewpoint.

It enabled me to develop my practice in aerial photography and discover the creative possibilities offered by onboard capture systems.

### 4. Developing my editing skills with DaVinci Resolve

I used **DaVinci Resolve**, as well as other tools, to explore and practice video editing.

This activity led me to work on transforming recorded sequences into structured audiovisual content.

It allowed me to address the main dimensions of postproduction:

- Sequence selection.
- Media organization.
- Building an edit.
- Pacing and transitions.
- Preparing the final render.

Editing is an essential step to transform footage into a coherent production.

### 5. Exploring immersive delivery with Meta Quest

I discovered the possibilities offered by virtual reality for viewing 360° content.

Using a Meta Quest headset allows watching immersive films by freely directing the gaze within the recorded environment.

This experience allowed me to understand the differences between traditional video, presented in a fixed frame, and immersive content where the viewer chooses the direction of observation.

It complements my exploration of 360° capture technologies with an approach centered on delivery and user experience.

### 6. Extending these activities with a YouTube channel project

My experiments in photography and video also led to a YouTube channel project.

This approach aims to have a space for presenting and sharing audiovisual productions.

It constitutes a natural extension of recording and editing activities.

## My Role

**I personally undertook these photographic and audiovisual experiments to develop my skills in image technologies.**

My activities include:

- Use of an Insta360 camera.
- Experimentation with kite-based photography.
- Aerial photography using drones.
- Video editing practice using DaVinci Resolve and other tools.
- Exploration of 360° films in virtual reality with Meta Quest.
- Development of a YouTube channel project.

## Technologies and methods

**Equipment:** Insta360 camera, drone, kite, Meta Quest headset.

**Software and platforms:** DaVinci Resolve, photo and video processing tools, YouTube.

**Technical fields:** aerial photography, 360° capture, immersive video, video editing, postproduction, virtual reality.

**Methods:** experimentation, shooting, media selection, editing, audiovisual delivery.

## Results

- Experimentation of several photographic and video capture techniques.
- Use of an Insta360 camera in various contexts.
- Exploration of aerial photography using a kite and a drone.
- Development of practical video editing skills.
- Discovery of immersive delivery with a Meta Quest headset.
- Commitment to content valorization through a YouTube project.

## Lessons learned

### 1. The capture setup transforms creative possibilities

A 360° camera, a drone, or a kite offer different viewpoints and lead to exploring new ways to compose an image.

### 2. Shooting represents only part of the audiovisual work

Processing and editing are essential to select images, build a sequence, and produce exploitable content.

### 3. 360° video renews the delivery experience

Unlike traditional video, the viewer can choose the direction of their gaze, which changes how images are perceived.

### 4. Experimentation allows understanding technologies

Handling equipment and software provides a concrete understanding of their capabilities and constraints.

### 5. Distribution constitutes a complementary step to creation

A YouTube channel project invites reflection on presentation, organization, and sharing of achievements, beyond their technical production.

**This project illustrates my technological curiosity and my ability to explore a complete audiovisual chain, combining aerial photography, 360° capture, digital editing, and virtual reality.**
