# Design in 3D and complete renovation of a house with SketchUp

## About the Project

I undertook the complete renovation of my house, including masonry, electrical, flooring, and interior fit-out works.

Beyond the execution of the works, this project required rethinking space organization, anticipating transformations, and designing future layouts.

**I used SketchUp to model the house in 3D and prepare the various envisaged transformations.**

This approach allowed me to combine digital design and physical realization in a concrete project.

## Objectives

- Design the house transformations and layouts.
- Visualize spaces before works execution.
- Study the placement of interior elements.
- Prepare renovation interventions.
- Coordinate the different project stages.
- Execute or have executed the necessary works.
- Fit out spaces and integrate furniture.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Model the house with SketchUp

I used SketchUp to create a three-dimensional representation of the house and work on layouts.

3D modeling allowed me to visualize volumes, study interior organization, and represent projected transformations.

This step served as a design support to prepare the renovation.

### 2. Design interior layouts

Starting from the digital model, I worked on space organization and placement of various elements.

The goal was to have a coherent vision of future layouts before starting works.

SketchUp served as a representation and preparation tool to align layout ideas with the building's physical constraints.

### 3. Prepare renovation works

The project covered several technical areas:

- **Masonry:** interventions on the structure and interior spaces.
- **Electrical work:** tasks related to electrical equipment and installations.
- **Floor coverings:** renovation and finishing of surfaces.
- **Interior fit-out:** organization of spaces and integration of furniture.

The diversity of these interventions required an overall project vision and coordination between the different stages.

### 4. Carry out the renovation and fit-outs

I led this personal renovation project by aligning design choices with the work executed.

The approach consisted in progressively moving from digital representation to the physical transformations of the house.

Personal interventions and those potentially entrusted to third parties can be distinguished more precisely in detailed site documentation.

### 5. Integrate furniture and finishes

The project also included the fit-out of spaces and furniture.

3D design enabled reflection on volume usage and the integration of interior elements within the house's overall organization.

## My Role

**I designed the fit-outs using SketchUp and led this personal complete renovation project (1 year of work).**

## Technologies and Methods

**Software:** SketchUp.

**Technical fields:** 3D modeling, building renovation, masonry, electrical work, floor coverings, interior fit-out, furniture.

**Methods:** digital design, spatial visualization, work preparation, project organization, practical execution.

## Results

- Design of fit-outs using SketchUp.
- Execution of the complete house renovation.
- Work involving multiple trades.
- Application of an approach linking digital modeling and physical execution.

## Lessons Learned

### 3D modeling facilitates preparation for transformations

SketchUp allows representing spaces and visualizing fit-outs before their execution.


### Design must account for real constraints

A digital model serves as a reflection tool, but decisions must remain compatible with the building's physical characteristics.

### Execution completes the design

Moving from modeling to work allows confronting design choices with their implementation.

**This project illustrates my ability to use a 3D design tool to prepare and lead a concrete transformation, combining technical reflection, organization, and practical execution.**
