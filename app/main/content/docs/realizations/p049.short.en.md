## The project

Listen before acting...

In a steel plant, performance does not depend solely on production capacities. Finishing, repair, and cutting operations also play a decisive role in the availability of finished products and their shipment.

A complementary industrial capacity for repair and cutting constituted a bottleneck in end-of-production flows.

## My contribution

I led the cross-functional Working Group, bringing together technical, operational, and organizational dimensions.

## Results

- Reduction in unplanned stoppages and control of maintenance budgets.
