# Analyze strip breaks and strengthen industrial risk control

## Context

On a continuous steel production line, a strip break constitutes a major incident that can lead to prolonged downtime, production losses, and complex equipment interventions.

These incidents can result from different mechanisms: traction instability, strip deviation, guidance defects, abnormal roller behavior, or a combination of several phenomena.

**Objective: Understand break mechanisms, identify at-risk situations, and capitalize lessons to improve process control.**

## Approach

The analysis relies on event reconstruction and cross-referencing multiple information sources:

- **Process data**: evolution of speeds, tractions, torques, and operating parameters before the break.
- **Event chronology**: identification of anomalies, regime changes, and sequences preceding the break.
- **Field observations**: examination of strip condition, equipment status, and operating conditions.
- **Technical analysis**: formulation and confrontation of hypotheses on failure mechanisms.
- **Lessons learned**: formalization of findings and identification of preventive measures.

Several incidents were thus subject to specific analyses, particularly regarding traction behavior, strip deviations, guidance equipment, and rollers.

The approach prioritizes understanding each sequence rather than the systematic search for a single cause.

## Achievements

**Incident Analysis**

Exploitation of operating data to reconstruct conditions preceding breaks, identify anomalies, and formulate technical hypotheses.

**Formalization of lessons learned**

Drafting structured incident reports centered on facts, analyses, and envisaged countermeasures.

**Improvement of Operational Practices**

Contribution to the formalization of operating rules and procedures in degraded situations to better control break risks.

**Integration into Risk Analysis**

Participation in the Process FMEA approach, integrating lessons from incidents into risk evaluation and the evolution of certain operations.

## My Role

As Process & Quality Manager, I led the technical analysis of incidents and structured lessons learned. Many events were experienced on call, enabling early collection of critical information to identify root causes.

My interventions focused on:

- Operations and interpretation of process data.
- Reconstruction of failure sequences.
- Drafting technical analyses and incident reports.
- Formalizing recommendations and operational rules.
- Participation in Process FMEA work.

This work was conducted in collaboration with production, maintenance teams, and process specialists.

## Results

- Development of technical analyses documenting multiple strip breakage mechanisms.
- Formalization of incident reports and actionable lessons learned.
- Contribution to the evolution of risk control operational rules.
- Integration of lessons from incidents into Process FMEA.
- Development of a structured approach linking industrial data, field observations, and failure prevention.

## Lessons Learned

The challenge is not only to understand why a breakage occurred, but to **transform each incident into reusable technical knowledge to durably enhance process reliability**.

Collecting facts while hot is critical as it prevents re-interpretation by stakeholders and allows identification of true root causes.
