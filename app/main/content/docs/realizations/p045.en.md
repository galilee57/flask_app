# Mastering the manufacturing conditions for new coated steels

## About the Project

The evolving requirements of the automotive industry drive the development of steels with increasingly demanding mechanical characteristics and coating properties.

On a metal coating industrial line, manufacturing these products requires adapting operating conditions to the specificities of each family: very high-strength steels, hot stamping steels, thick-coating grades, or differentiated zinc coatings.

These productions impose specific constraints on bath composition, product sequencing, transitions between steel grades, wringing parameters, and coating defect control.

**Objective: contribute to defining and mastering the industrial conditions required to manufacture these new products while respecting their quality requirements.**

## Approach

The approach combines production preparation, analysis of industrial trials, and capitalization of acquired knowledge.

It revolves around three complementary axes.

**1. Define manufacturing conditions**

- Analysis of specific requirements for new product families.
- Identification of metallurgical constraints and process limits.
- Definition of programming and sequencing rules.
- Preparation of transitions between product families.
- Formalization of the conditions necessary to produce the most constrained products.

**2. Exploit trials and industrial productions**

Trials and production campaigns allow comparing theoretical conditions with the actual behavior of the process.

The analysis focuses on:

- Line operating parameters.
- Coating and wringing conditions.
- Process stability during transitions.
- Characteristics and quality of produced items.
- Anomalies encountered during manufacturing.

Observations progressively feed the understanding of favorable conditions for each product family.

**3. Analyze defects and capitalize**

High-zinc-coating productions illustrate interactions between process parameters and coating quality.

Analyzing defects such as drips, zinc shortages, or edge overloads helps identify the manufacturing conditions associated with their occurrence.

Cross-referencing field observations with production data helps identify sensitive parameters and guide technical investigations.

## Achievements

**Formalization of industrial constraints**

Contribution to drafting technical documents defining programming and transition rules for high-strength steel families.

**Analysis of manufacturing conditions**

Utilization of production information to understand specific constraints for complex products and interactions between line parameters and achieved quality.

**Analysis of coating defects**

Contribution to a defect analysis approach and associated process conditions, particularly regarding issues encountered during high-zinc-coating-weight productions.

**Knowledge capitalization**

Structuring and sharing of technical rules and lessons learned to facilitate production preparation and control of industrial constraints.

## My Role

I contributed to formalizing manufacturing conditions for steel products with high constraints, in line with process requirements and programming needs.

This work is part of a collective approach involving Process, Quality, Production, and Programming teams.

## Results

- Formalization of programming rules for several high-strength steel families.
- Contribution to controlling sequencing and transition constraints between products.
- Capitalization of knowledge on manufacturing conditions for complex products.
- Utilization of technical analyses linking certain coating defects to manufacturing parameters.
- Participation in an industrial approach aimed at making production conditions more reproducible.

## Lessons Learned

Industrializing a new product is not just about demonstrating its feasibility; it requires defining the conditions that allow for controlled and reproducible manufacturing.

Mastering new products relies on the integration of metallurgical expertise, process knowledge, data analysis, and formalization of industrial practices.
