# Centralize and rationalize data to ensure reliable quality reporting

## About the Project

In a complex industrial environment, production and quality teams relied on numerous information sources: Excel files, Business Objects reports, industrial system extractions, and specific tracking dashboards.

This data was often distributed across different files and locations, with updates performed separately.

This organization presented several difficulties:

- The multiplication of files and reporting media.
- The need to update multiple sources to disseminate the same information.
- The risk of divergence between different versions of the data.
- The time spent searching, consolidating, and sharing information.
- The difficulty in identifying reference data.

**The objective: rationalize the organization of industrial data by centralizing existing sources and facilitating their dissemination to users.**

## Approach

### 1. Identify and organize existing sources

I contributed to a centralization approach for the information used for industrial tracking and quality analyses.

The scope included notably:

- Excel tracking and analysis files.
- Business Objects (BO) reports.
- Extractions from various industrial systems.
- Data and indicators used by business teams.

The challenge was to structure a set of heterogeneous resources to simplify their consultation and use.

### 2. Rationalize updates

The multiplication of media imposed repetitive maintenance operations.

The approach aimed to limit these redundancies by organizing information around common sources, rather than maintaining a multitude of independent files.

This rationalization pursued three objectives:

- Reduce multiple updates.
- Limit divergences between media.
- Facilitate the reuse of information in different analysis contexts.

**The guiding principle was to maintain a common piece of information rather than several copies evolving separately.**

### 3. Structure information dissemination with SharePoint

A dedicated **Microsoft SharePoint** space was used to centralize and disseminate resources.

It served as a common access point for the files, reports, and extracts necessary for users.

This organization enabled:

- Grouping resources in a shared environment.
- Simplifying their location and consultation.
- Organizing their availability.
- Facilitating sharing between teams.
- Reducing the dispersion of work materials.

SharePlay played here the role of a documentation and dissemination platform, not as a Big Data ingestion or processing engine.

### 4. Improve operations on data by business units

Centralization also aimed to make information more easily exploitable for quality investigations and process monitoring.

By simplifying access to existing resources, users could devote more attention to data interpretation rather than their search or manual consolidation.

This approach thus constituted a first step towards a more structured and shared use of industrial data.

## My Role

As Process & Quality Manager, I rationalized the data within my scope.

This project mobilized both my knowledge of industrial processes, my understanding of data, and my ability to organize their collective exploitation.

## Results

Reduction in operations and update time (a few minutes per day)
Adoption of MS collaborative tools by production teams

## Lessons Learned

**The valorization of industrial data does not necessarily start with new technology: it often starts with better organization of the information already available.**

Three principles emerge from this experience:

- **Centralize before multiplying tools**: a coherent organization of sources allows limiting the dispersion of information.
- **Reuse rather than duplicate**: maintaining multiple copies of the same information creates unnecessary burdens and risks of inconsistency.
- **Facilitate access to data**: the value of information also depends on users' ability to find it and exploit it.

**This project illustrates my ability to rationalize a heterogeneous data environment, organize the sharing of industrial information, and improve reporting practices in service of business units.**
