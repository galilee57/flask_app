## The project

Reconcile industrial diversification, production constraints, and control of investments.

## My contribution

I contributed to the feasibility study and preparation of line evolution scenarios, leveraging my process expertise and knowledge of operations constraints.

This project required me to integrate process, equipment, works, scheduling, and investment dimensions within an industrial decision-making approach.

## Results

**A finalized feasibility study, enabling an informed decision on line evolution.**

**The project was ultimately not implemented on the studied line, primarily due to the excessive impact of works on production downtime duration.**
