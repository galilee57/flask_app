# Regional Maintenance Manager

## About the Experience

This role marks an evolution in my career toward cross-functional responsibilities, with a more holistic view of industrial performance.

The challenge was to balance **facility reliability, budget control, maintenance process optimization, and skills development**, while taking into account the operational constraints of the various sites.

## Scope and Responsibilities

- **Economic Leadership:** Development and monitoring of maintenance budgets, allocation of credits, and prioritization based on industrial challenges.
- **Facility Reliability:** Steering maintenance policies to improve equipment availability and anticipate failure risks.
- **Methods and Organization:** Process improvement, harmonization of practices, and sharing of lessons learned between sites.
- **Shutdown Preparation:** Coordination and anticipation of technical, human, and material needs to secure planned interventions.
- **Skills Development:** Identification of critical expertise, support for teams, and encouragement of cross-functional collaboration.

## Challenges and Issues

The main difficulty of a regional responsibility lies in finding a balance between numerous operational needs and necessarily limited resources.

It is about **prioritizing actions based on risks and industrial challenges**, while building coherence among organizations with their own constraints.

This role also requires evolving practices without imposing solutions disconnected from the field, by involving teams in improvement initiatives.

## Lessons Learned

**Moving from operational to strategic vision:** Taking a step back on technical issues to understand their organizational causes, anticipate needs, and embed decisions for the long term.

**Learning to arbitrate:** Understanding that performance does not simply consist of reducing costs, but in engaging resources where they deliver the most value, while respecting safety and security requirements.

**Unifying without necessarily having hierarchical authority:** Developing the ability to convince, seek compromises, and build shared directions with operational teams.

**Maintaining the link with the field:** A method or organization is only truly effective if it meets the needs of the teams responsible for implementing it.

## What I Retain

This experience allowed me to broaden my understanding of industrial performance, integrating more economic, organizational, and human dimensions.

**Maintenance performance does not rely solely on technical expertise, but on the ability to anticipate, make the right trade-offs, and unite stakeholders around common priorities.**

> The dispersion of teams required the massive use of digital tools … 15 years before they became mandatory during the COVID-19 pandemic!  
>
**This practice profoundly changed my previously negative view of these tools, fully integrating them into my MINDSET.**
