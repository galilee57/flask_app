## The project

Prepare and coordinate temporary infrastructure, access routes, and services required for the successful execution of a major maintenance shutdown.

## My contribution

**As the maintenance engineer responsible for construction logistics, I coordinated the preparation, provision, and follow-up of the means necessary for the operation of shutdowns.**

## Results

Logistical arrangements were prepared and made available in strict adherence to the scheduled deadlines.

The temporary infrastructure required for the site was set up, including offices, parking spaces, and utilities.
