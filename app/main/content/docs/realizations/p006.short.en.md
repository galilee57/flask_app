## The project

Prepare and manage maintenance shutdowns.

## My contribution

**As maintenance manager, I drove the vision, secured the necessary financial resources, and led the preparation and execution of shutdowns.**

The detailed construction of the reference framework and the execution of interventions relied on the collective work of teams and mobilized contractors.

## Results

Shutdowns were executed within allocated budgets, notably thanks to savings achieved on services.

The detailed description of procedures enabled more precise tracking of interventions, from preparation to execution.
