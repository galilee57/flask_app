# Digitalize and industrialize training pathways with AI

## Context

Knowledge transfer in companies often relies on in-person training, presentations, or documents whose dissemination and appropriation remain difficult to track.

Training large-scale employees requires proposing accessible, homogeneous content adapted to different learning rhythms, while having the means to evaluate their understanding.

**Objective: design digital training pathways combining multimedia content, Artificial Intelligence, assessments, and learning tracking.**

## Solution

I designed and implemented digital training pathways integrated into an LMS (*Learning Management System*) platform.

The device combines several complementary components:

- **Educational pathways**: progressive organization of knowledge into structured modules.
- **AI-generated videos**: use of virtual avatars to present and explain content.
- **Audiovisual production**: editing, enrichment, and finalization of videos with Clipchamp.
- **Interactive assessments**: creation of quizzes to verify understanding of covered concepts.
- **LMS platform**: centralization of content and autonomous access to training.
- **Usage analysis**: operations on consultation, progress, and success statistics.

This architecture allows combining content production, dissemination, assessment, and tracking within a single device.

## Achievements

**1. Pedagogical Design**

- Definition of learning objectives.
- Structuring content into modules and sequences.
- Organization of pedagogical progression.
- Adaptation of messages to target audiences.

**2. AI Video Production**

Creation of video content using Artificial Intelligence-generated avatars (Synthesia) to present concepts and accompany explanations.

Use of Clipchamp to perform editing, integrate visual supports, and finalize pedagogical sequences.

**3. Assessment Creation**

Development of quizzes associated with modules to control understanding of content and identify concepts requiring further elaboration.

**4. Integration into the LMS**

Organization and publication of educational resources on the training platform:

- Structuring learning paths.
- Integration of videos and supporting materials.
- Linking quizzes to modules.
- Making content available for autonomous learning.

**5. Analysis of learning data**

Exploitation of the indicators available in the LMS to track:

- Content consultation.
- Progression through learning paths.
- Completion of assessments.
- Quiz results.

This information enables identification of usage patterns and guides the evolution of educational content.

## My Role

I was responsible for designing and implementing the various components of the digital training solution.

My interventions covered:

- Structuring learning paths.
- Designing content and training sequences.
- Creating videos with AI avatars.
- Audiovisual editing using Clipchamp.
- Developing quizzes and assessments.
- Integrating resources into the LMS.
- Exploiting usage and progression statistics.

This approach combines instructional engineering, digital tool mastery, and data exploitation.

## Results

Over 400 trainees across 3 learning paths!

## Lessons Learned

**Digitizing training does not simply mean converting a presentation into a video: it requires designing a complete learning experience, from knowledge delivery to assessment.**

Generative AI enabled the creation of reusable and adaptable content, facilitating better learner appropriation.
