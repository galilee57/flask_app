# Develop a sustainable weightlifting practice through discipline and learning

## Context

I have been practicing weightlifting for about ten years, with a usual rhythm of four sessions per week.

Over the years, this activity has become an important component of my personal organization.

Beyond physical training, I sought to deepen my knowledge of weightlifting and nutrition principles by following various trainings.

This approach combines regular practice with a willingness to understand the methods and knowledge that accompany it.

## Objectives

- Maintain regular physical activity over time.
- Integrate weightlifting sessions into my weekly schedule.
- Develop my knowledge of training methods.
- Deepen the principles of nutrition applied to sports practice.
- Maintain a learning and continuous improvement approach.
- Reconcile sports practice, personal life, and professional activities.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Establish a regular practice

I gradually incorporated weightlifting into my personal habits.

My practice spans about ten years, with a rhythm of four weekly sessions.

This regularity relies on long-term commitment and the space given to training in my schedule.

### 2. Develop my weightlifting knowledge

I did not limit myself to practicing exercises.

I also followed trainings to deepen my understanding of weightlifting.

### 3. Train in nutrition principles

I completed this learning with trainings dedicated to nutrition.

### 4. Inscribe the activity for the long term

The main characteristic of this project is its continuity.

Maintaining a practice over several years requires dedicating time to it and integrating it into your personal organization.

This experience allowed me to develop an approach based on constancy and perseverance.

## My Role

**I personally built and maintained this sports practice, while investing in my training to deepen my knowledge.**

My commitment includes:

- A weightlifting practice for about ten years.
- A usual rhythm of four sessions per week.
- Completion of training courses in weightlifting.
- Completion of training courses dedicated to nutrition.
- The durable integration of this activity into my personal organization.

## Methods and Knowledge Areas

**Areas:** weightlifting, physical training, nutrition, self-learning.

**Transferable Skills:** discipline, regularity, time management, perseverance, autonomy, continuous development of knowledge.

## Results

- About ten years of weightlifting practice.
- A frequency of four weekly workouts.
- Training completed in the fields of weightlifting and nutrition.
- A sports activity durably integrated into my lifestyle.

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Regularity is a long-term commitment

An activity practiced over several years requires constancy and an organization compatible with other responsibilities.

### 2. Discipline allows maintaining a personal approach

Regularly dedicating time to an activity contributes to developing perseverance and the ability to uphold a commitment.

### 3. Practice benefits from being accompanied by knowledge

Training courses enrich the understanding of a discipline and nourish reflection on one's practice.

### 4. Learning is not limited to professional life

The will to train and understand also applies to personal activities.

**Ten years of weightlifting, at a rate of about four sessions per week, illustrate my ability to maintain a long-term personal commitment by combining discipline, organization, and the will to learn.**
