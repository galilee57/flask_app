# Reduce surface defects related to dust on an industrial line

## Context

Controlling surface cleanliness is a major issue in the manufacturing of steel products intended for demanding applications.

An industrial production line encountered surface defects identified under the name "Black Spots", associated with deposits of dust and particles.

These defects were part of the topics followed within the quality improvement and process robustness program.

A specific working group was constituted to address this issue.

**Objective: understand the conditions for the appearance of black spots and identify levers to reduce surface defects and internal non-conformities.**

## Approach

The work was structured around three axes of reflection.

**1. Characterize the quality problem**

Distinguish "Black Spot" defects from other surface anomalies to have a clear definition of the phenomenon studied.

**2. Search for appearance mechanisms**

Examine possible interactions between the operating conditions of the line, its environment, and the presence of dust or particles likely to affect surface quality.

**3. Identify and implement corrective actions**

Ad-hoc: cleaning of the environment (nearby building, machine covers)
Regular: scheduling cleaning and monitoring of cleanliness status by production teams during stops
Investments: securing in-line equipment reliability, correcting their design to evacuate particles while preventing them from redepositing on the product.

## Results

- Reduction of Black Spot defects by a factor of 5 over the period
- Collateral effects on other defects with common mechanisms.

## Lessons Learned

An installation evolves over time: customer requirements change, maintenance dead ends/regular simplifications (such as TPM) pay off over time, design errors can have consequences years later.

If necessary, a one-off restoration is not enough: it must become a new benchmark for the manufacturing teams.

Often, minor investments are sufficient to provide the signs and means needed to improve a line over time.
