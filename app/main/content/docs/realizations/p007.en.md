# Commission a gas production unit

## Context

I held the role of **project manager for the construction and commissioning of a gas production unit**.

The project required translating final client specifications into an operational industrial facility by coordinating multiple stakeholders: the client, construction teams, and the future local operator.

My scope covered leading the construction phase, coordinating interfaces, and commissioning up to the delivery of the facility.

The project also faced technical challenges, including sizing errors that required restarting certain phases and caused delays.

This experience allowed me to gauge the importance of collective validations at critical stages of an industrial project.

## Objectives

- Coordinate construction teams and the local operator.
- Ensure consistency between client expectations and technical choices.
- Lead construction progress.
- Organize the commissioning phase.
- Maintain clear communication with the client.

## Approach and implementation

### 1. Translate client needs into industrial design

I led exchanges with construction teams to translate client specifications into design choices.

This step required ensuring that proposed technical solutions matched expressed needs and the operational constraints of the facility.

The objective was to maintain a permanent link between client expectations and decisions made during the project.

### 2. Coordinate stakeholders

I managed the project at the interface of three main actors:

- **The end client**, holder of requirements and recipient of the facility.
- **Construction teams**, responsible for technical studies and implementation.
- **The local operator**, concerned with future usage and operating conditions of the unit.

My role consisted of organizing exchanges, advancing decisions, and maintaining a common understanding of objectives.

This coordination was essential to prevent design, operations, and client satisfaction constraints from being treated separately.

### 3. Manage the construction phase

I monitored the progress of the installation implementation in coordination with the new works teams.

Managing this phase required handling interfaces between stakeholders and ensuring consistency of works with expected specifications.

This phase also highlighted the importance of validating technical choices before committing to difficult-to-modify steps.

### 4. Manage sizing errors

During the project, several sizing errors were identified.

They required restarting certain phases to correct the relevant technical choices.

These restarts resulted in delays to the project schedule.

I had to integrate these difficulties into project management, coordinate necessary exchanges, and explain the situation to the client.

### 5. Maintain transparent communication with the client

The delay caused by technical restarts needed to be explained to the end client.

I ensured this communication by presenting the difficulties encountered and their consequences on the project.

### 6. Manage commissioning and delivery

I participated in managing the commissioning phase, intended to prepare for the unit's startup.

This step constituted the transition between installation implementation and its operations.

It required close coordination with technical teams and the local operator.

The project concluded with the production start-up of the unit, in accordance with client specifications.

## My Role

**As project manager, I managed coordination of construction, interface management, and commissioning of the gas production unit.**

Detailed design and technical implementation were handled by specialized teams, which I coordinated within the framework of the project.

## Technologies and Methods

**Fields:** industrial gas production, facility construction, new works, commissioning, startup, industrial operations.

**Methods:** project management, specification analysis, stakeholder coordination, progress tracking, technical deviation management, choice validation, client communication.

**Skills:** managing industrial projects, cross-functional management, client relations, interface management, problem-solving, delay management, and startup.

## Results

### Commissioning of the Facility

**The gas production unit was commissioned and put into production in accordance with client specifications.**

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Client orientation must remain present at every stage

The project does not limit itself to building a technically functional facility.

It must meet client specifications and the actual conditions of its operations.

### 2. Commissioning must be anticipated from the design phase

Commissioning is not merely a final formality.

Operator needs and operating conditions must be integrated early enough to facilitate the transition to production.

**This project taught me that a successful industrial facility relies not only on the quality of its design, but also on the rigor of validations, stakeholder involvement, and transparency in management.**

Compliance with client specifications during production constituted the culmination of this approach, despite technical challenges encountered during implementation.
